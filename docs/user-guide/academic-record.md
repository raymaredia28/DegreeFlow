# Academic record

Your **academic record** is the ground truth that every degree evaluation is
run against. It lives on the Planner tab and looks like a list of terms with
a course card per row.

## What counts

A course contributes toward your degree only if:

- The **Count toward degree** checkbox is checked, and
- The grade is passing (D- or better for most requirements, C- or better for
  major/core courses where the university requires it), and
- The course is in the "completed" or "in progress" list (planned courses on
  the Planner grid do **not** count toward the current evaluation — only
  toward the *projected* state).

## Editing terms and courses

- **Drag** a course card to move it between terms.
- **Trash icon** on a card removes it from the record.
- **Add Course** inside a term lets you manually enter a course (useful for
  courses that didn't parse, or for pre-TAMU credit).
- **Add Term** at the bottom adds a new empty term.

!!! warning "Unsaved changes"
    Local edits do not take effect in evaluations until you click
    **Update Record**. If there are unsaved edits, a yellow banner appears
    across the top of the app (on every tab) with an **Update Record**
    button and a shortcut back to the Planner.

## Equivalent courses

DegreeFlow automatically recognizes certain equivalent course groups — for
example **ACCT 209 ↔ ACCT 229** and several MATH substitutions. If you
replace one with the other in your record, the evaluation uses whichever
satisfies the relevant requirement. See
[evaluation engine](../developer/evaluation-engine.md) for the exact list.

## Clearing your record

The **Clear Record** button wipes the stored academic record (and your saved
planner). Sign in again and re-upload your transcript to start over.
