# Planner

The Planner gives you a semester grid for **future** terms. Drop courses into
semesters to produce a projected graduation path.

## Layout

- **Left rail:** your past/in-progress terms (the academic record).
- **Main area:** future terms — one column each, with course cards stacked
  vertically.
- **Search drawer:** open with the **Add Course** button in any future term.

## Adding a course

1. Click **Add Course** in a future term.
2. Search by subject + number (e.g. `CSCE 411`) or by title.
3. Results show credits, prerequisites, and current availability flags.
4. Click a result to drop it into that term.

## Prerequisite checks

Each planned course is continuously checked against your record *plus* earlier
planned terms. If prereqs are not met, the card shows an amber or red badge
with the unmet prerequisite; hover to see details.

- :material-alert-circle-outline: **Missing prereq** – block shown in amber
- :material-close-circle-outline: **Conflict** – block shown in red (e.g.
  the course is only offered in a term you've skipped)

## Credit-hour guardrails

A term with more than 18 credit hours highlights in red. The evaluator also
respects the per-term limit when projecting completion.

## Autosave

Planner changes save automatically to your account. Unlike the academic
record, you don't need to click "Update" — moving a card persists it within
a second or two, and the UI shows a "Saved" pill when the write succeeds.

## Fullscreen view

Click the **Fullscreen** button at the top-right of the planner to maximize
the grid when working with many terms.
