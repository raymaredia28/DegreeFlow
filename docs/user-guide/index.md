# User guide

DegreeFlow is organized around five screens that roughly mirror the workflow
of planning a CS degree:

1. [**Academic record**](academic-record.md) — the authoritative source of
   what you've taken, what's in progress, and what transfer credit counts.
2. [**Planner**](planner.md) — a semester-by-semester grid where you drop
   future courses.
3. [**Degree evaluation**](evaluation.md) — the rollup that tells you which
   requirements are complete and which are still missing.
4. [**Prerequisites viewer**](prerequisites.md) — an interactive graph of
   the courses and their dependencies.
5. [**Chat assistant**](chat-assistant.md) — a conversational helper that
   can suggest, add, or remove planned courses.

Admins also have access to a [course-catalog administration panel](admin.md).

---

## Suggested first-time flow

If it's your first visit, follow the pages in order:

1. [Sign in & upload a transcript](getting-started.md)
2. [Review and clean up the academic record](academic-record.md)
3. [Plan upcoming semesters](planner.md)
4. [Run a degree evaluation](evaluation.md)
5. (Optional) [Ask the chat assistant for suggestions](chat-assistant.md)

## Saving your work

DegreeFlow autosaves planner changes but **not** academic-record changes. Any
time you edit the academic record (e.g. delete a course with the trash icon
from the planner's term cards), a yellow banner appears at the top of the app
reminding you to click **Update Record** before re-running evaluations.
